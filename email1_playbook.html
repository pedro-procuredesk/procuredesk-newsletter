THE ONE TIP MOST PEOPLE MISS

Don't read the report top-down by total. Scan the over-90 and over-120 columns first — that's money already at risk, and those are the vendor conversations worth having today, not next month.

The summary at the top totals every aging bucket automatically.

The 4-step setup

01

Open it in Excel or Google Sheets

Works in both. Start on the How to Use tab — it's a 30-second orientation.
02

Make it yours

Add your company name and reporting period. Only type in the blue cells — the rest are formulas.
03

Enter each invoice

Vendor, invoice #, dates, amount, and amount paid. Balance, days overdue, status, and the aging buckets fill themselves in.
04

Work the report

Scan over-90 first, prioritize those vendors, and let the summary at the top show you the totals at a glance.

Three columns worth understanding

Credit Terms

Net 30, 60, or 90 — how long you have to pay. It's what sets the due date.

Days Overdue

How far past due each invoice is. This is what drops a balance into the right aging bucket.

Status

Paid, Partial, or Unpaid — set automatically from invoice amount vs. amount paid, so it's never out of sync.

The built-in How to Use tab walks through every cell.

A few numbers worth watching

Once your invoices are in, these are the figures that tell you how healthy your payables really are:

Total outstanding — everything you owe across all vendors, right at the top.

Outstanding by aging period — how much sits in each bucket. A heavy over-90 column is your warning light.

Balance per vendor — who you owe the most to, so you know where one conversation moves the needle.

Number of overdue invoices — the count that actually needs action this week.

Average days to pay — how fast you settle once an invoice lands. If it's creeping up, something upstream is slipping.

That's the whole workflow. Fill it in once, and the template handles the math from here on out.
